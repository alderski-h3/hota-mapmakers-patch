Changelog

1.1.0 — 2026-11-19

  • Adapt objects and rivers: A new Terrain tool mode that updates scenery, compatible objects, and rivers as you change terrain.
  • Connected Fill: A new Terrain brush that paints a connected region of terrain.
  • Ellipse Brush: Draw ellipses with the Terrain tool. Hold Shift to draw a circle or Alt to draw from the center.
  • Rectangle Brush: The Area Brush has been renamed. Hold Shift to draw a square or Alt to draw from the center.
  • Regenerate Objects: A new Obstacle tool action that refreshes existing scenery in the marked area and updates compatible object appearances.
  • Connected Replace for roads and rivers: Keep drawing while the brush replaces connected roads or rivers it touches. Matching river deltas change too.

1.0.0 — 2026-11-15

First public release for HotA 1.8.1.

  • Pathfinding compares lowest travel cost and earliest arrival, with movement profiles and waypoints.
  • Battlefield Explorer previews and searches battlefield layouts. Visibility previews player coverage, patrol areas and single-cast Dimension Door range.
  • Advanced Find searches supported map contents and text. Randomness Inspector shows possible artifact and spell pools.
  • Multi-Brush mirrors, rotates or offsets strokes around a pivot, with up to 36 placements per custom rotation including the original. One Undo reverses the whole stroke.
  • Editing tools include map area copy, cut and paste, object ordering, terrain and image tools, and clearer monster quantity, hero portrait and calendar dialogs.
  • The optional launcher checks for signed updates to the patch DLL and both launcher files. It does not update Heroes III, HotA or HD Mod.
